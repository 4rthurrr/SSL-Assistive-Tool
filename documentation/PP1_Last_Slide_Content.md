# PP1 Presentation - Last Slide Content
**Section:** Entire Project / Sustainability

Here is the tailored content for your final slide.

### 1. Commercialization / Sustainability of Solution
*   **Sustainability model:** A **Freemium** approach ensures accessibility for all, while premium add-ons enable financial viability.
*   **Commercialization:** Deployment through a **web-based SaaS platform**, making it easily accessible to schools and homes without complex hardware.

### 2. Customer Persona
*   **Primary User:** Deaf & Hearing-Impaired **Children (Ages 5-12)** who struggle with reading Sinhala.
*   **Buyer/Decision Maker:** Parents seeking educational tools and **Special Education Teachers** in government/private deaf schools.
*   **Secondary User:** General public learning Sign Language to communicate with deaf peers.

### 3. Market Size
*   **Target Market:** Approx. **400,000+** members of the Deaf community in Sri Lanka.
*   **Expansion Potential:** The "Concept-ID" architecture is scalable to **Tamil Sign Language (TSL)**, opening up Northern/Eastern Sri Lankan markets and potentially South India.

### 4. What is Unique in Your Solution?
*   **Semantic Concept Engine:** Unlike direct translators, our system understands *meaning* (e.g., distinguishing "Mama" from "Mata"), solving the Diglossia reliability issue.
*   **Culturally Adaptive 3D Avatar:** A specialized local avatar (Sri Lankan school uniform) instead of generic western robot avatars, increasing child acceptance.
*   **Multi-View Output Verification:** 3-Mode output (Skeleton/Normal/AI) allows users to inspect the sign structure clearly, aiding ensuring accuracy.

### 5. How will the cost be recovered?
*   **B2B Licensing:** Annual licensing fees for **Special Education Schools** to use the "Learning Games & Analytics" dashboard.
*   **B2C Subscriptions:** A small monthly fee for parents to unlock "Advanced Vocabulary Packs" and "Story Mode" features.
*   **Grants/CSR:** Partnering with telecommunication providers for CSR-funded data-free access packages.
